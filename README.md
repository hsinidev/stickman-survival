### Game Details & Guide
Stickman Survival: A Comprehensive Guide



Game Overview


Stickman Survival is a browser-based game that challenges players to survive as a stickman in a procedurally generated world.



How to Play


The objective of the game is to navigate through a maze, avoiding obstacles and collecting resources while managing your stickman's health and hunger.



Controls:


To play the game, simply click on the Stickman Survival icon in your browser to start playing. You can use the arrow keys to move left or right, and the space bar to jump.



Objective


The goal is to survive for as long as possible while managing your stickman's resources, health, and hunger.



Tips and Tricks for Success:



    

Crouch when you have low health to avoid damage from enemies and obstacles. This will help you conserve your energy and increase your chances of survival.


    

Collect all the resources (gold, food, and health) available in each level. This will give you a better chance of making it through the game.


    

Use your surroundings to your advantage. Look for hidden paths and secret areas that can help you escape danger or find new resources.


    

Don't be afraid to use your health or food if necessary. Using up all your resources will result in a game over, so use them wisely.






Why You Should Play It:


Stickman Survival is a fun and challenging browser-based game that offers many benefits, including:



    

A unique and addictive gameplay experience that will keep you engaged for hours.


    

A procedurally generated world, ensuring that each playthrough is different and full of surprises.


    

Endless resources to collect, making it easy to spend hours exploring the game's vast world.






Promotions and Updates:


The game has received regular updates with new levels, content, and features. Players can expect a constant stream of new challenges and obstacles to overcome.
